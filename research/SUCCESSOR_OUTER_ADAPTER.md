## Completion and ownership

Success requires an observed zero child exit, joined reader threads, closed
pipes, agreeing narrow terminal records and unchanged request/source/packet
receipts. The parent independently verifies all eleven dependency artifacts,
both bridge envelopes, controller start and exact stdout summary. Success also
requires the child cleanup metrics and both helper/role-loader closures.

A recorded child refusal requires a valid bound refusal envelope and an observed
child exit of 2. It remains `needs_review`. Missing or contradictory envelopes,
timeouts, unknown process completion, altered sources or parent cleanup failures
remain unknown. Neither state permits automatic retry or rollback. A source
closure may be verified while completion remains refused or unknown.

Mounting the ordinary loader is inside its cleanup ownership. A stored-then-
interrupted mount still gets one release attempt, and a freeze error survives a
simultaneous close error. Child artifact verification occurs while its helper
owner is active. Terminal validation and publication preserve the child's
original failure. Parent helper ownership spans loading, supervision and saved
verification; final release is attempted once. Publication is never retried.
