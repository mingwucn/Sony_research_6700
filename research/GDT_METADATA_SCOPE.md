result, verify the new source seal/reviews/qualification, freeze exact metadata

before invocation. Preserve collisions and partial files; never replace them.

only reviewed AST-selected constants. It does not read the firmware source, so

Use **two planned `inspect_local` calls**, each with its own normal source open
and **two same-descriptor full-source passes**: preliminary capture/hash, rewind,
then full decoded-stream validation and parent hash. These are dependent phases
of one future operation, never a retry of a failed phase. No random-access reopen,
one-pass shortcut, speculative descriptor retention or alternate hypothesis.

1. **Phase A: validate and plan.** Reconcile the complete source identity, logical
   stream/header/spans, full opaque-link directory, all five original prefixes
   and expanded outcomes, and both fixed-superblock observations with the frozen
   historical basis. Retain only the two fresh fixed prefixes within their byte
   allowance until this reconciliation completes. Then call the unchanged
   planner stage 1 once per lead. Freeze its bounded exact primary-GDT spans,
   bound to private member identities and current extents. Clear phase-A raw
   prefixes before phase B. A refusal is terminal `needs_review`; no descriptor
   collection or second pipeline may begin.
2. **Phase B: collect and validate metadata.** Open only the same pinned package
   under a cross-phase path/state handshake. Perform the full inherited
   validation again. Collect only the frozen primary-GDT spans while streaming;
   these bytes remain provisional until the complete phase-B lineage matches
   both phase A and historical records. Retain fresh fixed prefixes for exact
   pairing. Only then invoke planner stage 2 once per lead on those fresh
   prefixes and exact descriptor buffers. Validate all rows; do not accept a
   prefix of the table. Clear raw references on every exit.

The cross-phase handshake must bind source observable state and complete hashes,
member identity/extent, directory equality, fresh-prefix hashes and all accepted

opens and matching hashes do not create an atomic snapshot or authenticate Sony
