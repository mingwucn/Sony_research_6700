[session report](sessions/20261020-RA-003-isolated-current-preflight.md) for final
qualified source identities, exact commands, results and limitations.

invented isolated outer operation before `prepare_modules`, observer construction,
record reservation or factory callbacks. Original gate documents, the six retained
basis records, historical inputs and prepared request bytes must agree. The gate
retains its non-JSON qualification log as raw bytes; it is checked against the
original candidate hash/size rather than incorrectly treated as parsed JSON.

Comparison source paths are relative to the candidate fixture root, whereas the
prepared adapter paths are relative to the repository root. An explicit pair of
absolute roots maps the retained paths to the same lexical locations. Raw basis
records are preserved; the conversion neither opens sources nor proves freshness.

Two closed detached metadata graphs share the existing loader: fourteen comparison
helpers and three current-preflight helpers. The existing three-helper adapter

state are checked around calls; mutated owner buffers are rejected. The isolated
child executes captured loader/audit bytes with a restricted importer rather than
resolving ambient `tools` modules. These checks assume the reviewed interpreter,
stdlib and bootstrap; they are not a sandbox or proof of complete host integrity.

The process source map grows from 48 to 61 labels. The prepared source map stays
at 46 labels and 41 unique paths. Thirteen added bootstrap/preflight labels do not

A detached JSON result binds original document hashes, current ordinary-record
hashes, request hash, comparison seal, source roles and the explicit path frames.
The child saves that result exclusively and places its hash in the terminal record.
The parent checks the saved bytes and closed result schemas against its original
packet before accepting preflight evidence. Rebinding an outer result hash cannot
make contradictory comparison evidence pass. No hash constitutes human consent.

Each acquired metadata owner closes independently, including failures during load
or call. The child records once-only close attempts for both owners. Early
metadata refusal must leave zero preparation, record, stream, cipher and factory
attempts. Later phase failure stays refused; altered saved evidence stays unknown.
Both outcomes are needs_review, with no automatic retry or rollback.

wait, 1-second reader join and 2MiB per-pipe cap. The qualification harness has a
separate 100-second subprocess limit. Real process exit, joined reader threads and
closed pipes establish cleanup; a polling timeout alone does not.

All pipeline inputs are invented, including the stream, constants and dependency

no retained original record is rewritten to make integration pass. Supplied source

Public runtime entry points and direct CLIs remain disabled. No installed crypto
activation, actual firmware/constants/native payload read, new actual activation
record, camera command, feature support, recovery proof or device-write gate.

step; English resources, onboard FTP, exact-body correspondence and recoverability
