## Required binding sequence

1. **Source and activity review.** Review the successor source closure, intended
   act, startup grammar, private input roles, limits and evidence thresholds.
   Candidate/decision/review records must bind the same operation and exact
   source/request bytes. A review hash or boolean does not create human consent.
   Historical reviews, the old source cache and earlier dependency-only results
   cannot authorize or replace the current act. No actual records are assembled
   in this specification.
2. **Ordinary parent startup.** Verify the selected interpreter/profile and
   externally anchored closed records; reject unknown/extra input roles before
   opening them. Pin request/packet/source identities and exact child argv.
   Keep source text, package/config/proof metadata and actual constants/firmware
   distinct. Actual firmware/constants must not appear among ordinary reads.
3. **Fresh disposable child startup.** Validate isolated/no-site/no-user-site,
   no-bytecode and unoptimized CPython, exact interpreter/profile/argv, clean
   relevant native/crypto aliases and bounded source/packet bytes. Validate the
   complete source closure before authored execution. Keep role planning and
   ordinary basis validation before the dependency session. The old authority
   gate's closed input roster and legacy reference pins need an explicit delta;
   neither schema relabeling nor an invented review literal is sufficient.
4. **Current runtime packet.** Bind `runtime_dependency_packet_v1` to original
   expectation, package and root identities, exact config/cache text, new review
   binding and source closure. Use `bind_runtime_packet`; exclude invented
   contents, vectors-to-cipher substitutes and observer objects. Saved prior
   cache/config records may be historical evidence only. Do not fabricate fresh
   receipts from them.
5. **Engine-owned runtime dispatch.** The future reviewed wiring has a closed
   signature and passes no observer, loader substitute, backend or packet-defined
   continuation. Its authored call is the controller's internal `_run` with
   `invented=False` and no `ops`; that calls execution's internal
   `_produce_collected_runtime`, which constructs `producer.RuntimeOps()` after
   startup checks. The continuation is engine-owned. Public controller and
   wiring `run_runtime` currently refuse and remain unchanged in this package.
   Private Python functions are trusted composition seams, not authority checks.
6. **Single ownership and protected preparation.** The caller owns records until
   exclusive controller-folder reservation succeeds. Then the bridge owns one
   close attempt and callback release. Do not stack legacy `run_child_owned`.
   The current collected transcript is durable and reverified before its single
   callback claim; the same AES owner and immutable anchor reach the factory.
   Only that continuation may prepare actual constants and the three protected
   roles. Preserve guard/finder ownership through both phases, release and final
   vector checks. Failure is sticky even if a callback catches an exception.
7. **Parent reconciliation.** Keep the existing bounded supervisor's exact stdout
   hash, semantic summary and terminal-record agreement. Use the checked-buffer
   parent graph to recompute all eleven dependency artifacts, both bridge
   envelopes and the independent start record, in saved runtime-record mode.
   Validate operation, original expectation, complete source/packet freshness,
   namespace/role cleanup and controller completion together. Runtime-shaped
   saved records establish consistency, not an observed actual run. A narrow
   controller success cannot dominate an outer refusal or unknown result.

## Refusal, ownership and unknown completion

Before reservation, refusal must leave records with their caller; after transfer,
record/release attempts stay single-owner and durable attempt markers precede
ambiguous actions. Preserve the primary failure across cleanup errors. A failed
completion publication is not repeated. Exclusive folders and outputs cannot be
overwritten or replayed. Preserve partial artifacts for review.

The retained supervisor has a 300-second deadline, two MiB per log, one bounded
termination/wait path and bounded reader joins. Reconcile its actual terminal
observations rather than assuming a child exited. If supervision throws after a
known handle was returned, retain its PID and `unknown_after_supervisor_exception`
without extra polling, killing, waiting or launching. No automatic replay,
camera reboot or rollback follows an uncertain outcome. Future qualification
must cover changed startup/roster/input mode, claimed transfer then setup failure,
callback refusal, release failure, missing/changed envelopes, incomplete journals,
deadline behavior and known-handle cleanup failures.

Do not widen the pure saved-controller success verifier to call a failure
successful. Outer failure reconciliation needs its own closed refusal/unknown
grammar, preserving whether reads, controller entry, cleanup and terminal exit
were observed. `needs_review` is an outcome requiring inspection, not permission
to start a new operation. The invented adapter's refusal cases are evidence for
that adapter only; they do not qualify the future operational refusal grammar.
