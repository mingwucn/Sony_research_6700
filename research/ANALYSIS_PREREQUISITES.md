group-descriptor tables consistent with their fixed superblock metadata and
the existing structural predicates? A GDT describes filesystem layout; this
study would help prepare later navigation toward language resources or FTP code.

whole source before validating the decoded stream. Together that means four
source passes totaling **4,096,071,392 bytes**, with at most **65,536 descriptor
bytes** retained. These passes are justified within the chosen method; no claim
is made that this is the only possible method. Root, inode, bitmap, directory and
content selection remain outside this study. Failure stops the sequence; phase B
is not a retry. Existing journal, timeout, byte-bound and cleanup rules remain.
