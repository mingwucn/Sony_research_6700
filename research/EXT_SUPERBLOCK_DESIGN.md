## Pure observer contract

Accept an exact built-in `bytes` prefix of at most **4,096 bytes**, and an exact
built-in positive `int` complete member extent at most **2^63 - 1 bytes**.
Reject bools, subclasses and invalid length/extent relationships with a fixed
input-contract diagnostic. The extent cap is a project policy, not an ext format
limit. Parse only the fixed primary superblock at the documented member-relative
location. A complete superblock needs **2,048 prefix bytes** including preceding
padding; shorter input is an explicit insufficient-input observation.

Return closed scalar metadata and bounded fixed reason codes. Distinguish absent
magic, insufficient bytes, internal geometry contradictions, and readable but
unsupported revision/feature/layout combinations. Preserve raw source-defined
fields separately from conditional effective values. Respect revision-zero
defaults, conditional high counts and descriptor size; do not call ignored fields
corruption. Guard logarithms before shifts. Do not allocate or iterate by any
untrusted count. Integer calculations must remain bounded by parsed field widths
and explicit policy, with member extent checked before a geometry result is usable.

The source-backed detailed contract covers **41 fixed scalar fields** and **16
modeled predicates**. Unknown revisions retain raw fields but omit all effective
and derived geometry. Free/reserved-count sanity, inode-table fit and placement,
first-inode versus total-count consistency, group-number semantics and other
uncited checks remain unvalidated. A ceiling inode-table block count is descriptive
arithmetic only. Matching modeled predicates or a classic feature-mask policy
does not establish eligibility for a future planner or complete Linux acceptance.

Record geometry such as block/inode counts, block/group sizing and descriptor
variant only where the pinned sources justify its interpretation. A conservative
